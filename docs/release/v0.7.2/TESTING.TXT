NewTOS 0.7.2 - How to test
==========================

(Deutsch: siehe TESTANLEITUNG.TXT)

Simple: use NewTOS the way you would use an Atari. There is no checklist.
Most bugs show up during normal work and trying things out - that is
exactly what we need.

Some ideas if you don't know where to start:

- Work with the desktop: windows, folders, copy, rename and delete files,
  settings, "Save desktop".
- Try the programs: QED (write texts), zView (look at your own pictures),
  Doom, TeraDesk, CoreMark, HypView (open QED.HYP).
- Open the accessory from the Desk menu while something else runs.
- Restart with Ctrl+Alt+Del.
- Everything you know from a real Atari: does it look the same, does it
  behave the same?

New in 0.7.2, please try:

- Speed: open, close, move and resize windows, scroll long texts in QED
  and HypView, open folders with many files, look at pictures in zView.
  Does it feel faster than 0.7.1?
- Does everything still look the same as in 0.7.1? The new drawing
  routines must draw exactly the same pixels. Watch for wrong colours,
  missing or extra lines, gaps in text, leftovers after moving a window.
- The mouse pointer: move it slowly and fast over the desktop, icons,
  menu bar and windows; open menus and choose entries, drag icons, drag
  a selection frame, move and resize windows, drag scroll bar sliders;
  click buttons and text fields in dialogs. The pointer should follow
  smoothly and leave no traces or holes. While a program is busy
  (e.g. Doom starting): does the pointer keep moving, or does it stand
  still until the program is ready? Please tell us which.
- Use the mouse for a few minutes: no hang, no freeze.

What we are especially interested in:

- Crash report (text with "pc=", registers and a diagnosis block at the
  end): please take a PHOTO of the whole screen, then press a key.
- Hangs: what did you do just before?
- Anything that looks or feels different from an Atari with TOS 4 /
  EmuTOS - small things too.

Report as a GitHub issue (https://github.com/tuxie01/NewTOS/issues ->
New issue -> Bug report); FEEDBACK-EN.TXT lists what helps us.
Thank you!
