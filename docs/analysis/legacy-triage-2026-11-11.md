Dated snapshot, read-only research for [LEGACY01](../../TASK/LEGACY01.md). No ticket and no receipt was changed; drafts are in `TASK/migration/` and carry no approval line. Historical after its date. This second version applies the owner answers of 2026-11-11 (section 2); batches 1, 2, 3 and 7 are byte-identical to the first version (1, 2, 3 approved by the owner).

## 1. Method

- Scope: every key of `tools/xtask/ticket-baseline.tsv` (677 distinct keys = 656 ticket files + 21 orphan-receipt keys without a ticket file). Every key is in exactly one class.
- Facts per ticket: line 3 of `TASK/<ID>.md`; the direct receipt `TASK/<ID>-STATUS-DONE.MD`; commits from `git log` whose subject or body names the id (word boundary); hashes inside the receipt and the status line (current, or pre-rewrite translated through `docs/commit-map.txt`); measurement runs cited by the ticket.
- Open clauses are read from the status line and the first 8 lines of the receipt; a clause stated only in the ticket body is not seen.
- Batch format (TOOL54, from the lead): digits-only `<N>`, header line `id<TAB>state<TAB>fields<TAB>evidence`, fields as `Priority: Pn | Key: value`, no approval line. A row without a usable target, Priority or commit is a `# UNDECIDED` comment after the header.
- **Verification of a commit against its diff (Q3, Q4, informational for Q1/Q2).** A commit counts as VERIFIED only if one of these holds, each read from `git show --name-only`: (a) the commit names the id in its subject and touches a code file (or, for research tickets, a document) that the receipt or ticket cites; (b) the commit names the id (subject or body), is a `fix`/`feat`/`perf`/`test` commit of at most 100 files that touches code, and its message names a function or constant from the ticket title (identifiers with an underscore or 6+ capitals); (c) for research and measurement tickets, the commit first added a measurement run or report document whose path carries the id or whose subject names the id, or it is the commit that added the DONE receipt itself (the report) and is neither bookkeeping (`chore`, `docs(task)`, `docs(status)`) nor a bulk commit over 100 files; (d) the commit adds the report the ticket cites. Receipt-named hashes alone are not accepted (receipts often cite the base commit). Unverified candidates remain in the row as the placeholder `Commit` and the evidence says `NOT VERIFIED` and why.
- Everything automatic was then read by hand for the samples (section 6); seven title matches were rejected by hand (A101, A82, FS06, V16, A48, A93, DESK50).
- No QEMU or Hatari run. M3 rests on committed tables only.
