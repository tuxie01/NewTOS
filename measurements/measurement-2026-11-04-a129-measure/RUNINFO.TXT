PIXEL FACTS (NewTOS head, absolute screen coords; EmuTOS the same relative to its origin)
  FSVSLID track: black column x=978 and x=1003 (the 1-px OUTSIDE border, ob_spec thickness byte 0xFF = -1), black rows
  y=560 (top) and y=652..653 (bottom + the down arrow's own top); inside x=979..1002, y=561..651 the IP_4PATT
  checker, black where (x+y) is even. EmuTOS: x=338 / 363, y=260 / 3 rows below, same checker, same (x+y)-even
  parity relative to the origin. FTITLE: outline top y=476, bottom y=497, left x=842 (the closer box's right edge)
  and right x=1003 black; checker inside, black where (x+y) is even; col 1003 solid black on every row, col 1002 pure
  checker.
  So next to each outline column the checker has black pixels in every second row: border + adjacent checker pixels =
  a 2-px strip of 75 % black against the 50 % mean, left and right (top/bottom rows get it from the border row).
  That is the "dark strip"; there is no extra/odd column anywhere.
COMPARE
  FILEAREA 181x199 px, head vs EmuTOS: 192 differing pixels, ALL in x=88..111 (relative), y=3..18: the "*.*" text in
  FTITLE that EmuTOS draws and NewTOS does not (see out-of-scope note in TASK/A129.md). Everything else -- outline,
  checker phase, closer, arrows, thumb (with the list at top 0), box edges -- identical.
  pre (bcffca60) vs head, whole 1920x1080 screen: the only differing pixels lie in (979,558)-(1003,561) = the thumb's
  lower end (A128 changed the thumb geometry); FTITLE and the track's left/right edges are identical.
