          On the Pi 4 every USB 1.x/2.0 device reaches the VL805 through its internal hub 2109:3431 on root port 1
          (root ports 2..5 are the USB 3 protocol ports), so "blue vs black jack" for these devices means a
          different downstream port of that hub.
