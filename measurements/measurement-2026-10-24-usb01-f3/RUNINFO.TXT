  * The multi-function path against hardware: one Configure Endpoint with two/three interrupt endpoints on the
    VL805 behind the hub TT (Bandwidth/Resource Error -> fallback line), SET_PROTOCOL per function, events of
    several DCIs of one slot, the receiver's mouse moving the pointer and its keyboard typing (P2).
  * The per-function keyboard state against the real symptom (cable keyboard + receiver, P3); in QEMU the
    input route cannot be aimed at one of two keyboards (USB02 item 7), the mechanism is proven by H-2 only.
  * Load with a 1-ms mouse (Rii X8, P4); gamepad regression (P5, host test only).
  * P1: our Pi set-ups (Apple keyboard 05ac:020c behind its hub + ROCCAT 1e7d:2cee) have no second boot
    interface (checked in the logs of usb01-con04-pi / -pi-kbdhub / -snap-pi), so their commands and lines are
    expected unchanged -- to be confirmed on the Pi.
