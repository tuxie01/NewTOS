WHAT / WHY
  Iorec returned its function number 0x0E for every device; the classic flush
  io->ibufhd = io->ibuftl ended in a page-0 bus error (XBIOS-CHARDEV-001, S1). Implemented
  per the user decisions E1-E6 (docs/api/xbios/0e-iorec.md, "Entscheidungen", and the new
  section "NewTOS implementation"): one shared page 0x7D000, EL0 RW, with the three records
  (LP64 _IOREC, device 0 EXT_IOREC 56 bytes, oracle values); the IKBD record replaces
  RX_RING for every producer and consumer; 32-bit entries with the shift state at push time
  (BIOS11); the kernel checks the record before every use and after every trap, repairs and
  warns once per process; kbdvec hook x1 = Iorec(1).
