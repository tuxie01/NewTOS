WHAT QEMU DOES NOT EXERCISE (the real Pi does)
  - The xHCI/USB-HID path and INPUT01's timer-interrupt capture of USB reports (QEMU raspi4b has no PCIe/VL805; the
    injector feeds crate::ikbd::feed from the trap-side pump instead, kernel/src/host_env.rs:500@pump_devices).
  - Real Cortex-A72 caches/TLB/ASID behaviour (MEM12 switches page 0 RO/RW for every kbdvec upcall,
    tos-api/src/bdos/process/superstate.rs:40@set_super).
  - The user's real card contents (accessories, desktop) -- here: NEWDESK #Z autostart + two SYSACC.NAC.

WHY A FREEZE WITHOUT SERIAL IS NOT A DIAGNOSIS
  A kernel panic and every unhandled exception report ONLY on UART0 and then halt the CPU
  (kernel/src/panic.rs:17@on_panic, kernel/src/arch/aarch64/exceptions.rs:2428@unhandled); on board-rpi4 UART0 is off
  without `serial=on` (kernel/src/console.rs:51@SERIAL_TX_DEFAULT) and nothing is drawn on screen. The last DOOM frame
  stays -- indistinguishable from a hang. Also, while DOOM's hook is installed every key byte goes to DOOM's ring
  (tos-api/src/xbios/kbdvecs.rs:350@route_key), so a stuck DOOM swallows Ctrl+Alt+Del too.

POST-RUN
