WHAT / WHY
  BIOS-CORE-003: the kernel published its BDOS-internal 24-byte MD through Getmpb; a program reading
  through the SDK's LP64 _MD (32 bytes) got md_start = m_start | m_length << 32 and md_length = 0.
  Contract (docs/api/bios/00-getmpb.md "NewTOS-Vertrag [BIOS06]", V1-V5): the header's layout, one
  static snapshot [membot, memtop), only mp_mfl/mp_mal written. Before the change, O3: what EmuTOS
  does with Getmpb(NULL).
