WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE FIRST RUN
  `tools/xtask/src/doc_check.rs` `check`: under --staged a failure in a staged doc counts only
    if it stands on a line new since HEAD (a broken cite, an unknown ADR, an unanchored line cite

    resolves at HEAD (ADR cite: whose ADR HEAD has); checked with a second resolver on the HEAD
    tree (`Resolver::use_tree(&Tree::Rev)`). Everything else is legacy: counted in the summary
    ("N legacy failures ignored"), never fatal. Docs the commit leaves alone: unchanged rule.
