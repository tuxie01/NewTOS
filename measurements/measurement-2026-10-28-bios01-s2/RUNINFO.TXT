WHAT / WHY
  EmuTOS: a logical Rwabs write starting at sector 0 arms blkdev[dev].forcechange
  (bios/blkdev.c:452-453) before the range and media-change checks; the write itself then fails
  E_CHNG and writes nothing unless RW_NOMEDIACH (blkdev.c:473-477); every logical Rwabs on the drive
  returns E_CHNG, RW_NOMEDIACH included (blkdev.c:450-451), and Mediach 2 (blkdev.c:802-803), until
  Getbpb clears it (blkdev.c:590). NewTOS answered 0 everywhere (TOOL07: W01-W05, X13, X14 DIFF;
  TOOL07b: x004 39, x005 39/50, x006 38 DIFF). Spec: docs/api/bios/04-rwabs.md (order table),
  09-mediach.md, 07-getbpb.md.

GEMDOS CONSEQUENCE (BIOS01 work item 4) -- probe method/gcprobe.c, same source both sides
  EmuTOS (source): the next GEMDOS access to the drive meets forcechange -- getbcb's Mediach on a
  buffer hit (bdos/fsbuf.c:179-190) or E_CHNG from the Rwabs of a miss (bdos/fs.h:60-68) -- and osif
  re-logs the drive in with Getbpb + log_media and restarts the call (bdos/bdosmain.c:448-482); that
  Getbpb clears forcechange. NewTOS's BDOS reads through bios::bcache directly, never through
  Rwabs/Mediach, and keeps a logged-in drive's geometry (bdos/fs/fat.rs Volume::mount), so it neither
  meets the state nor re-logs.

  Findings:
  1. Re-login: EmuTOS re-logs C: on the first GEMDOS access after the arming and clears forcechange;
     NewTOS does not. With this fix, Mediach and every logical Rwabs on C: stay 2 / E_CHNG after a
     GEMDOS access until somebody calls Getbpb (A07, B05: 2 vs 0). Before the fix they were 0 by
     accident (nothing armed). This window is new with the fix; its cause is the missing BDOS
     re-login, not the BIOS state.
  2. S1, OCCURS: after a program changes the boot sector's geometry, NewTOS's GEMDOS keeps the old
     geometry (B04) and WRITES WITH IT (B06): the file's cluster 3 goes to the sector that is cluster
     7 under the boot sector on disk -- a write into another cluster than FAT and directory name.
     Pre-existing (identical before and after this fix; BDOS layer: bdos/fs/fat.rs Volume::mount
     caches, no ckdrv/getbcb media-change path). Reported, not fixed (BIOS01 work item 4).
