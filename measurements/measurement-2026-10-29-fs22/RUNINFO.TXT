WHAT / WHY
  FS20 restarted a call after a media change only inside gemdos::dispatch. The kernel's own file
  paths -- Pexec's load (the trap handler, the kernel launcher, the accessory loader), the whole-file
  reads of bdos::fs::read_file (AES resources, shel_find, GEM.RSC, NEWDESK.INF, KEYBOARD.KBD), the
  directory listings (file selector, scrap, AUTO, accessories) and the shell's direct GEMDOS calls --
  failed on a pending change until the next GEMDOS call on the drive. In EmuTOS all of them are
  trap #1 calls under osif's restart (bdos/bdosmain.c:449-486; xexec's clean-up bdos/proc.c:319-332;
  docs/analysis/osif-restart-coverage-2026-10-29.md). User decision 2026-10-29: option B, one
  shared restart helper, per-path clean-up, a bound of the restart.
  Spec: docs/api/gemdos/_state.md section 4a ("The kernel's own file-system paths [FS22]", replacing
  "Open [FS20]"); deviation docs/COMPATIBILITY.md "A media change restarts a call at most three times".
