RESULT
  (a) WHICH PEN: the drop shadow -- NewTOS pen 8 (LWHITE) where EmuTOS draws pen 1 (BLACK, = W_BOX's border colour);
      after wind_set(WF_COLOR, W_BOX) EmuTOS's shadow follows the new border colour (RED), NewTOS's stays pen 8.
  (b) RGB OF A PEN: NewTOS = EmuTOS falcontc in all 16 pens (tosdiff 0/48). The A130 values 102 vs 140 and 170 vs 189
      are the TT's own default palette (tt_palette1) against the Falcon's (videl_palette1): same pen, other machine.
  (c) GEOMETRY: equal -- shadow, border, gadgets and bevels at the same pixels on both sides.
  Cause (code read after the measurement): tos-api/src/aes/window.rs draw_shadow fills both bands with the constant
  DROP_SHADOW_COLOR = GRAY_LT = 8 (window.rs:180), EmuTOS paints the SHADOWED decoration of W_BOX in just_draw with
  vsf_color(bcol) (aes/gemoblib.c:894-900), bcol = gr_crack of W_BOX's word 0x1101 (aes/gemwmlib.c:84 gl_waspec,
  :1284 W_ACTIVE[ROOT].ob_state = SHADOWED).
