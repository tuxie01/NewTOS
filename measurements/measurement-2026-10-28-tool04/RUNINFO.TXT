FINDINGS FOR TRIAGE (not fixed here)
 1. target/debug/deps grows by ~3 000 objects per tos-api edit and never shrinks; every rustc lists it at start
    (result 4). Remedies, each a lead/user decision: one-time removal of target/debug (host builds only; the kernel's
    target/aarch64-*/ untouched; cold rebuild of the host suite ~25 s) and/or a [profile.test] that stops the growth
    (result 5: split-debuginfo=packed keeps full debug info at 1.8 GB and ~13 s relink; =off is fastest but loses
    source lines in backtraces/lldb; incremental=false keeps debug info, no growth, ~19 s relink).
 2. The first bloated relink (446.6 s) took twice the second (211.8 s); mds was busy indexing the new files. Excluding
    target/ from Spotlight was not measured.
 3. Without the Developer Tools exemption (cargo started from VS Code, ssh, launchd/cron for a nightly run) the
    2026-10-27 numbers apply again: ~25-75 s per newly linked binary, ~2 h per tos-api edit, in either tier.
