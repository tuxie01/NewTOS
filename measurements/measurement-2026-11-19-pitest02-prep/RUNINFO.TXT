HOW THE OWNER SWITCHES BETWEEN THE DEFAULT BOOT AND vdiplanes=32 (cmdline.txt on the card), AND WHETHER THAT NEEDS THE MAC
  Default boot = CMDLINE.TXT empty (the card's state today: one byte, a line feed; the dry run reads it as "empty (no options)"; the kernel reads it through the VideoCore mailbox at every cold start, kernel/src/bootargs.rs:3-6).
  vdiplanes=32 = CMDLINE.TXT containing "vdiplanes=32". Two ways, both described in TESTBLATT.md, Teil 0:
    (1) with the Mac: cp -X "/Volumes/BOOT 1/CMD32.TXT" "/Volumes/BOOT 1/CMDLINE.TXT"  (back: CMD16.TXT) -- certain, needs the card reader and the Mac;
    (2) on the Pi alone: edit C:\CMDLINE.TXT in QED, save, power-cycle. NOT driven by me (no real Pi): so the Mac is needed only for the certain path; whether the
        on-Pi path works (QED saving into the boot partition's root, firmware re-reading after a cold start) is an open assumption of the sheet, labelled as such.
  The active setting is verifiable from the Pi's screen without a serial line: SCRNINFO.TXT says "16 planes" or "32 planes" (steps A2, B2).
