DESIGN (docs/TOS-REFERENCE-DUMPER.md section 6, "The cookie jar")
  A call table takes a second kind of case line, the jar case, in the call column:
    jar:<TAG>   the value of the first entry with that tag (as Getcookie finds it), or absent;
                retclass EXACT | NONZERO | PTR_RAM | ABSENT (new class, jar cases only)
    jar:*       the number of entries and the tags in jar order; retclass EXACT
  args and capture "-"; no prelude, not inside a Super bracket, not after Getmpb.
  No trap is issued (no whitelist entry): the runner reads the jar afresh per case through the
  SCRNINFO backend's td_jar_read (Supexec on the m68k, the per-process page 0 on NewTOS); both
  DUMPBIOS builds now link that backend.
  Record: the call record, no format change. trap 256 (TD_CALL_TRAP_JAR), opcode 0 tag / 1 jar:*,
  args[0] the tag, ret_hi:ret the value ZERO-extended or 0xFFFFFFFF_FFFFFFFF (absent; a present value
  has no high half, so a cookie 0xFFFFFFFF records as 0x00000000_FFFFFFFF), jar:*: ret = count, capture
  = the tags as big-endian LONGs (max 64). tosdiff judges a jar case with jar_check: EXACT between the
  dumps, NONZERO/PTR_RAM present + property per side, ABSENT absent per side; it prints "absent",
  "not in the jar", and both tag lists for jar:*. Not measured: the jar's capacity (terminator value).
