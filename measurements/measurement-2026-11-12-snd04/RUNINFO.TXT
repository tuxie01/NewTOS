Auditor (Opus), lead request 2026-11-12. Measurement + new probe; no product code, no commit, no reference re-recorded.
Audit report: docs/audits/2026-11-12-snd04-dmasound.md.

WHY
  TASK/SND04: cases O1-O5 of TASK/SND01.md "Spec review" section 4, semantics S1-S7 of
  docs/api/xbios/_dmasound-group.md section 9.1.2. S3 (Buffoper bit 0 at the end of a single-shot frame), S4 (Buffptr
  granularity) and the Sndstatus(1) source conflict are in no pinned source.
  THE ORACLE IS HATARI'S DMA SOUND MODEL, NOT SILICON: EmuTOS 1a7484ab drives Hatari 2.6.1's Falcon crossbar model
  (profile falcontc) and its STe/TT DMA sound model (profile tt). tools/hatari-oracle.sh runs Hatari with --sound off;
  the model still runs (counters advance, end-of-frame events reach the MFP), only host audio is off.

WHY A PROBE, NOT A TABLE
  DUMPBIOS' guard (tosdump/core/cases.c, call_policy) admits only Soundcmd(0|1|6,-1), Buffoper(-1), Sndstatus(0) and
  Buffptr(buffer) from this group; Locksnd, Unlocksnd, Setbuffer, Setmode, Settracks, Setmontracks, Setinterrupt,
  Dsptristate, Gpio, Devconnect and every set arm are refused as "not established". Editing the guard is outside the
  auditor's remit (it is tosdump). O2/O3/O5 are timing (end of frame, pointer progress over time, interrupts) that
  no case table expresses. So: a permanent probe, tosdump/probes/snprobe.c, like hkprobe.c.
