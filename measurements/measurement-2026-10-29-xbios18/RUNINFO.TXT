PROBE (tosdump/probes/axprobe.c; header comment = the full description)
  Kbdvbase(); in super state (Supexec) the kbdvec slot (Kbdvbase()[-1]) gets the probe's hook, a
  few lines of assembly per CPU: count the call, record the byte and _hz_200, chain to the old
  vector with the byte unchanged (m68k: d0/a0 untouched, push+rts as
  sdk/apps/doom/doomgeneric_gem_kbdvec.c; AArch64: x0/x1 untouched, tail call `br`, integer
  registers only). Bconstat(1)/(2) before; ready marker (C:\AXPROBE.RDY where there is a drive,
  "AXPROBE:WAIT" on the console); t0 = _hz_200; Bconin(1); t1 = _hz_200; one more second of
  Vsync(); hook put back. Compared lines:
    X01 Bconin(1) result          X02 hook calls in total
    X03 hook calls with _hz_200 >= 20 ticks (0.1 s) before Bconin(1) returned = DURING the wait
    X04 the hook's bytes          X05 Bconin(1) waited >= 200 ticks
    X06/X07 Bconstat(2), Bconin(2) (the key the chained default put into the BIOS queue)
    X08 hook put back
  I lines: raw tick distances, informational (emulator timing).
