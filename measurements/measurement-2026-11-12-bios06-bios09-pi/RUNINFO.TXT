2. BIOS11 -- not measured
  KPROBE.LOG: K11 Bconin(2)=001e0061 ('a', equal to the reference); K12-K16 "no key queued". Cause: the probe reads
  400 hz_200 ticks (2 s) after the first key (measurement-2026-10-29-xbios01/method/kprobe.c lines 41-45), built for
  injected keys; the test sheet wrongly said "pauses between keys under 2 s". A hand typist cannot give six keys in 2 s.
  Owner, asked whether to build a variant with a longer window: "3 nein schift und alles funktioniert doch,was müssen
  wir denn da noch testen ?" (no: shift and everything works). BIOS11 stays DONE on its QEMU/EmuTOS evidence; no
  real-Pi value for K16.
