  when the parked call is re-executed. Holder A deletes/opens/closes the window and ends its hold -> the lock is handed to B
  (UPDATE_GRANTED) -> B's re-executed call refuses and never reached update_beg -> lock stays (1, B).
  EmuTOS: the checks come before wm_update(BEG_UPDATE) (aes/gemwmlib.c:1415-1422, 1443-1450, 1632-1638); the woken process is the
  holder (unsync, aes/gemflag.c:121-122) and its body ends with END_UPDATE.
  Fix (3cb21e31): crysbind -- for 101/102/103/105 with the gate false, window::end_unused_grant(): collect a pending hand-over and,
  if it made the caller the recorded holder, unsync it (the lock goes to the next waiter, as after the caller's own END_UPDATE).
  A grant from wm_new's drain (no count) is only collected. Named difference (spec window-graphics.md "A refusal after the wait"):
  EmuTOS would run the body on the window it validated before the wait; NewTOS returns the refusal the table now gives. Same lock
  outcome.
