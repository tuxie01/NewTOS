OPEN
  - BIOS-STOR-004/-005/-006 have no tosdiff case. They need boot sectors that differ from the image
    (bps 1024, tot16 = tot32 = 0, spf 0): a patch step the dumper does not have, and a restore after
    a failed Getbpb, which EmuTOS allows only through the physical path (bios/blkdev.c:443-444, :601)
    -- whose unit number is the machine's (IDE 18 on the oracle, open on the Pi, BIOS-STOR-002).
    Stopped per the brief; options in the report to the lead.
  - BIOS-STOR-002 (RW_NOTRANSLATE) is refused by the guard for the same reason.
  - The disk canary (ORACLE section 6) still uses the derived bios-r1.tsv; TOOL06 foresaw "the
    non-writing cases of the disk round" -- not changed here (the brief: both canaries unchanged).
