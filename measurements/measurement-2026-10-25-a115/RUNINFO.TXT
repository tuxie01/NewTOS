4. FIX (tos-api/src/bdos/process/sched.rs, "The ready list")
  Membership stays Process::runnable; order = per-slot RL_KEY (position on rlr, appended = next counter value) and DRL_KEY
  (non-zero while on drl, push stamp), KERNEL_RL_KEY for the parked EL1 kernel client.
    ready(slot)   = disp_act (end of rlr)            unready(slot) = off rlr/drl (nrl, Pexec-suspended, gone)
    push_drl      = signal's push (only off nrl)     drain_drl     = schedule()'s drain, latest push first
    pick_head(cur)= drain + head of rlr (slot or kernel client); None when the head is the caller
    admit         = pstart (push onto drl)           wake/wake_timers push onto drl; wake_timers in dlr order
                                                     (deadline ascending, later arm first: TIMER_ARM_SEQ)
    sleep: unready (mwait_act), timers, pick_head; dsptch/dispatch_after_call: ready (disp_act), timers, pick_head;
    on_yield (Pwait): a ready caller goes to the end; pick_peer_for_kernel: kernel client appended, pick_head.
    suspend/resume_parent_of_child: unready / the parent takes the child's rlr place (one AESPD in EmuTOS).
  pick_next (slot order) and pick_after_call removed. Every writer of `runnable` goes through sched: process.rs free_slot
  (sched::unready), the 9 host-test fixtures and both unwired kernel selftests (selftest_sleepwake, _switch).
  Comment-only: kernel/src/arch/aarch64/exceptions.rs (Pwait, kernel park), kernel/src/accs.rs, tos-api/src/aes/acc.rs.
  Tests whose assertion encoded slot order, changed to the EmuTOS order with the reason in place:
    aes_gh1_update_mutex.rs  END_UPDATE hands the lock over: the waiter joins behind the holder; the holder's next AES
                             call gives it the CPU (was: the closing dispatch of END_UPDATE itself).
    sched_dsptch_admits_the_caller.rs / sched_dsptch_keeps_caller_ready.rs  accessories woken before the caller's dsptch
                             are on drl: the first dsptch keeps the caller (still admitted -- the property these test),
                             the second runs the head.
