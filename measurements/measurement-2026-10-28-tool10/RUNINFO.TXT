  the path; cmd_build prints "xtask: kernel8.img for the card: <path>" as its last line; cmd_run_rpi4 boots the

  in KERNEL8_IMG_LOCATIONS = [KERNEL8_IMG_RELEASE (target/.../release/kernel8.img), "kernel8.img"] and
  fresh_kernel_image takes the one candidate whose mtime is at or after the build start (whole seconds); none or
  two fresh = error. A stale root image in a post-TOOL10 tree is never measured. The report names the image path.
  Tests only_the_image_the_build_wrote_is_taken, kernel8_img_release_is_where_this_xtask_writes_it.
