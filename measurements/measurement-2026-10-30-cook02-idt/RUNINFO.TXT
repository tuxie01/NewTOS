WHAT / WHY
  Before: tos-api/src/cookie.rs:226@IDT seeded _IDT = 0x0000112F ('/' separator, DD-MM-YY, 24 h),
  commented as "bring-up policy". The oracle (EmuTOS 1a7484ab, COUNTRY=de, Hatari NVRAM) has
  0x0000112E ('.'); jar-r1 case 53 was OPEN in docs/COMPATIBILITY.md
  (docs/analysis/cook02-country-facts-2026-10-30.md section 4: the separator byte is the only
  difference).
  Decision (TASK/COOK02.md, user 2026-10-30, point 3): NEWDESK takes its date separator from _IDT
  when the date preference is "Standard" (programs/desktop/src/model.rs:194-201@rendered, like
  EmuTOS references/emutos/desk/deskinf.c:140-145), so NewTOS seeds the oracle's 0x112E.
  Change: one value in seed_defaults (and its comments); _AKP untouched (stays 0, case 52 ACCEPTED).
  Host test tos-api/tests/gemdos_ssystem_idt_cookie.rs now asserts 0x0000112E and separator '.';
  the synthetic jars of sdk/cflib/tests/cookie-host.c and sdk/overlays/teradesk/tests/cookie-host.c
  carry the new value.
