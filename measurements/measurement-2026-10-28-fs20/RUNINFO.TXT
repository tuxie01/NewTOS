WHAT / WHY
  S1 (TASK/FS20, measured in measurements/measurement-2026-10-28-bios01-s2/RUNINFO.TXT, section
  "GEMDOS consequence"): after a program rewrites C:'s boot sector (Rwabs RW_NOMEDIACH), EmuTOS's
  GEMDOS meets the pending forcechange on its next buffer access and logs C: in again (osif,
  bdos/bdosmain.c:449-487); NewTOS's BDOS read past the BIOS media state, kept the old geometry
  and wrote a new file's cluster 3 into the sector that is cluster 7 by the boot sector on disk.
  Spec: docs/api/gemdos/_state.md section 4a [FS20] (new), from the EmuTOS source.
