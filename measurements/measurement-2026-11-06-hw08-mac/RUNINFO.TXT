HW08, write latency of two SD cards on the Mac (lead, 2026-11-06, user asked for a card benchmark).
Host only: tools/sd-bench.py on mounted volumes, temporary files, removed afterwards. No raw write.
This is NOT the Pi path: USB reader plus the macOS FAT driver. It compares cards; it does not show what the Pi sees.

WHY
  TASK/HW08.md: saving fails sporadically on the Pi until a reboot, saving and copying are slow at times;
  user suspects a card timeout. NewTOS waits 1000 ms for a write (WRITE_BUSY_MS, kernel/src/drivers/bcm2711/emmc.rs).

SETUP
  host_clang: Apple clang version 21.0.0 (clang-2100.4.1.1) (no image built, no compile; named by the run rule)
  Card A, test card: 15.6 GB, 4 x 500 MB FAT16, 8 KiB clusters, tested on NEWTOS3 (498 MB free).
  Card B: 63.9 GB, one FAT32 partition, 32 KiB clusters, almost empty, UNTITLED.
  Reader 1: Generic 3131:1017, USB 3. Reader 2: 6820:0048, USB 2, reports itself as "Generic HD camera".
  Card makers and CID are not readable through these readers.

RESULT  sd-bench-results.tsv, one line per run; the three runs of the same script version:
  Card A, reader 1: random 4 KiB over 256 MiB p50 998.65 ms, max 1990.9 ms; same within 256 KiB p50 8.58 ms, max 55.2 ms;
    small new files p50 37.84 ms, max 653.3 ms; 18 operations over 500 ms, 8 over 1000 ms.
  Card B, reader 2: random p50 5.25 ms, max 40.2 ms; 0 over 500 ms.
  Card B, reader 1: random p50 3.86 ms, max 37.6 ms; small new files p50 9.53 ms, max 40.0 ms; 0 over 500 ms.
  So reader 1 does not stall by itself. Card A was not measured in reader 2. A first run of card A, before the
  local test existed: random p50 996.13 ms, max 1996.4 ms; the line was replaced, its numbers are only here.

VARIANTS, card A, reader 1, ad hoc script, not kept, 64 MiB file, 14 s or 4000 writes each (8 s for the last), p50 / max ms:
  4 KiB random 64 MiB F_FULLFSYNC, F_NOCACHE       683.40 / 2002.1   n=26
  4 KiB random 64 MiB fsync, F_NOCACHE             985.21 / 1971.2   n=21
  4 KiB random 64 MiB F_FULLFSYNC, cached          173.24 / 2020.0   n=31
  8 KiB random 64 MiB F_FULLFSYNC, F_NOCACHE       524.98 / 1989.8   n=21
  8 KiB random 64 MiB fsync, F_NOCACHE             817.01 / 1985.5   n=19
  4 KiB random within 256 KiB F_FULLFSYNC            7.96 /   38.7   n=1778
  4 KiB same offset F_FULLFSYNC                      8.40 /   39.6   n=1403
  4 KiB sequential F_FULLFSYNC                       5.63 / 1005.1   n=1187
  64 KiB sequential F_FULLFSYNC                      1.61 /  996.7   n=1993
  4 KiB random 64 MiB no flush, F_NOCACHE          309.62 / 1986.7   n=11
  The stall follows jumping across the card, not the flush or the block size. Values near 1000 and 2000 ms are unexplained.

LIMITS
  Cards differ in file system, partition size and fill level; only one of the two was measured with each layout.
  Card B's "worst 1 MiB block 0 ms" shows the sequential write was buffered; its MiB/s still includes the final flush.
  A buffer in the reader could also shorten card B's latencies; the script cannot tell. Whether card A stalls on the Pi is open.
