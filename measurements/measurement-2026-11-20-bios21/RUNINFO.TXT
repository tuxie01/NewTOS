  (docs/api/bios/_sysvars-contract.md section 8). BIOS21 builds the case type that section proposes:
    peek:<addr>:<width>   one cell (1, 2 or 4 bytes) of $380..$7FF, read inside a Supexec callback on both machines;
                          read-only by construction; classes EXACT IGNORE NONZERO, at width 4 PTR_RAM and the new
                          MONOTONE (two reads with two Vsync calls between, the second later than the first)
  and the table tosdump/cases/bios-sysvars-r1.tsv: one case per row of class "served" of the contract table
  (51 rows, 54 cases: _themd as its four LONGs). Spec of the form: docs/TOS-REFERENCE-DUMPER.md section 6,
  "System variables: the peek case".
