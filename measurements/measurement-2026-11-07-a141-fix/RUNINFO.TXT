THE PATCH (sdk/overlays/hypview/rsc/, upstream sdk/apps/hypview/ unchanged)
  recolour-cicons.py recolour <classic in> <classic out>: per 8-plane CICON variant, each pixel of col_data and sel_data:
    register n -> pen REV_MAP_COL[n] (EmuTOS init_colors, references/emutos/vdi/vdi_col.c:736-747) -> nvdi.pal[pen]
    -> the register m whose TOS default colour tos-default.pal[REV_MAP_COL[m]] is nearest.
    NEAREST: smallest squared Euclidean distance over r, g, b in VDI units 0..1000 (the files' values, unweighted, unquantised),
    over all 256 registers; on a tie the lowest register.
  nvdi.pal: verbatim copy of references/freemint/xaaes/src.km/pal/nvdi.pal @ f906416 (sha256 15b4b326...).
  tos-default.pal: written by `recolour-cicons.py tos-pal kernel/src/vdi_backend.rs ...` from VIDEL_PALETTE1/2
    (kernel/src/vdi_backend.rs:56@VIDEL_PALETTE1, :70@VIDEL_PALETTE2), equal to EmuTOS videl_palette1/2 (vdi_col.c:130-199; checked
    in Python here and by the host test). Origins and hashes: sdk/overlays/hypview/rsc/palettes.sha256.
  sdk/apps/build-hypview.sh: recolour into build/hyp_view-recoloured.rsc (log build/recolour-cicons.log), then tools/mkrsc.py convert
    of that into build/hyp_view.rsc.
