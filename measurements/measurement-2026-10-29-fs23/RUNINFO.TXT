  14 bytes of d_fname, zero-filled after the name (tos-api/src/bdos/fs/find.rs:465@makbuf before the

  else (references/emutos/bdos/fsdir.c:1297-1324, makbuf :1851-1862), so the bytes after the NUL keep
  what the DTA held before. User decision 2026-10-29: match it.

  Acceptance (ticket): the F1 cases of gemdos-mc-fs22.tsv and gemdos-mc-paths.tsv EQUAL to the
  oracle; no previously equal case of fs20, fs22, paths, bios-r1 newly differs; cargo xtask test green.
