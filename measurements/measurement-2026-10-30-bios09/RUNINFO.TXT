measurement-2026-10-30-bios09 -- Bconin(1)/Bconin(6) wait on board-rpi4 like EmuTOS bconin1
(TASK/BIOS09, audit finding BIOS-CORE-008; user decision 2026-10-30, option (a))
=====================================================================================================
Developer, TASK/BIOS09, 2026-10-30 (host clock CET). Tree: main at f065a705 plus the uncommitted
BIOS09 change. QEMU raspi4b headless, -append serial=on. No hardware, no real card, nothing
flashed. Not committed.

WHAT / WHY
  Before: on board-rpi4 AUX (BIOS 1, and handle 6 through Bconmap, BIOS08) was the "port present,
  no cable" entry: Bconin(1) returned 0 at once (tos-api/src/bios/chardev.rs AuxNoCable), every
  serial byte the PL011 receive interrupt delivered was dropped (kernel/src/console.rs
  serial_rx_byte, not-qemu-virt branch). EmuTOS' bconin1 waits on its iorec
  (references/emutos/bios/serport.c:255-263, bconin_iorec :202-210); the audit's hang probe
  showed it (measurements/measurement-2026-10-28-bios-core-audit/runs/HANG-EMUTOS-tt-1, -6).
  Change: the qemu-virt AUX driver of XBIOS15/XBIOS18 (AuxSerial, aux_read_blocking, install_aux,
  routing into Iorec(0)'s input ring in serial_rx_byte) runs on both boards; the wait loop moved
  to tos-api (bios::chardev::aux_read, host-tested), the kernel keeps the DAIF/wfi around it.
  No hardware register change.

     Bconstat(1)/Bconstat(6) answer 0 as before (QEMU sends no serial byte).
  2. Hang probe, after (runs/BCHANG1-..., BCHANG6-...): "H0 after" present, "H1 after" /

     oracle's HANG-EMUTOS-tt-1 and HANG-EMUTOS-tt-6. Before (BIOS08's run
     measurements/measurement-2026-10-30-bios08/runs/BCHANG-NEWTOS-raspi4b-after): every "after"
     present, ret 0.

       W1 Bconstat(1)=0, waiting in Bconin(1)
