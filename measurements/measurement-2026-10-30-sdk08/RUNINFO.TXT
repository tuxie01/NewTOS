2. CHANGE (sdk/compiler-rt/tests/rt-host.sh, sdk/libc/tests/ldbl-host.sh)
  Step 2 compares with parity(): byte-identical -> "same"; otherwise both objects are copied
  through `llvm-objcopy --remove-section=.comment` and the copies must be byte-identical AND the
  two original symbol tables (`llvm-readobj --symbols`) must agree -> "ident"; else "differ"
  (FAIL). The symbol check exists because removing .comment also drops the symbols defined in it
  (AArch64's `$d' mapping symbol at .comment+0); without it the per-section counter-check below
  found one inverted symbol-table byte not reported (libc/arch__cmdargs.o, that `$d' symbol's
  st_value) -- fixed before this run. The step passes when nothing differs and same + ident = all.
  The step prints the host clang and the clang named by the shipped members, and runs a built-in
  counter-check.

     itself judges it. Expected "ident" for .comment only, "differ" for everything else.
