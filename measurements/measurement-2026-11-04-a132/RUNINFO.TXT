CAUSE OF THE 140
  FTITLE's TEDINFO colour word is 0x11A1 (aes/gem_rsc.c:37-40 in references/emutos), bit 7 set = MD_REPLACE
  (gr_crack, references/emutos/aes/gemgraf.c:621); just_draw passes it on (gsx_attr(TRUE, tmode, tcol),
  aes/gemoblib.c:709; the 3D switch to MD_TRANS at :582-599 needs a hollow white interior, FTITLE has IP_4PATT),
  and the VDI's replace-mode text fills each cell's background with white. NewTOS's AES text path writes every
  glyph WriteMode::Transparent (tos-api/src/aes/object.rs gsx_tblt; its own comment says the tmode switch has no
  counterpart). Fixing that is a change to the shared object text draw, every TEDINFO text with bit 7 set -- not
  part of A132 (named in TASK/A132.md "Stand Umsetzung").
