5 FINDING F1 (not fixed; product code untouched, for the lead)
  NewTOS writes the 200-Hz counter only into the page 0 of the RUNNING process, on each tick (tos-api/src/bios/tick.rs
  `publish`; besides that only the loader seeds it). A parent that gets control back after Pexec still reads at $4BA the value
  of its own last tick, from before the child ran, until its next tick (at most 5 ms); a process that runs for less than a
  tick between two children is never refreshed in between. On EmuTOS _hz_200 is one global cell. Measured: P0REP v1 timed
  each child with direct $4BA reads and saw no time for a 7.5-s key wait (runs/p0rep-release-inj-v1) and random "max"
  values (runs/p0rep-nowait-v1). P0REP v2 waits for the next tick around each Pexec (hz200_next_tick). HZPROBE starts no
  child, so F1 is outside its scope (stated in its header).
