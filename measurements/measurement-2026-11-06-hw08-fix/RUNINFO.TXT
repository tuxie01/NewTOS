7 POWER FAILURE, MEASURED, case n4: results/n4-emutos, n4-before, n4-after with the files as on the card
  Closed before: EXIST.DAT, OLD.DAT, 20000 B. Then, without any Fclose: NEW.DAT Fcreate + 5281 B; EXIST.DAT 100 B overwritten at 100 and
  3000 B appended; OLD.DAT 700 B overwritten at 512. Machine killed. Cards:
                           EmuTOS            before                  after
    NEW.DAT                0 B               0 B                     0 B
    EXIST.DAT length       20000             20000                   20000
    EXIST.DAT 100 B        on the card       on the card             LOST (dirty in one of 8 buffers; EmuTOS evicted it from 2)
    EXIST.DAT 3000 B       lost, length old  lost, length old        lost, length old
    OLD.DAT whole sector   on the card       on the card             on the card
    OLD.DAT tail 188 B     lost              on the card             lost
    fsck                   clean             too many clusters,      clean
                                             3 orphans
  So: nothing that had an Fclose is at risk. Before Fclose a new file was and is 0 bytes, an appended part was and is lost, on EmuTOS too.
  New is only: a partial sector overwritten inside an existing file stays in memory longer, up to 8 sectors per list, and is lost with
  the power until an eviction or the next flushing call. In exchange the FAT on the card is never ahead of the directory.
