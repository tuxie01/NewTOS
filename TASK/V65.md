Title: VDI 32 planes: 32-plane memory MFDB: `vro_cpyfm`, `vrt_cpyfm`, `vr_trnfm`, `v_get_pixel`, screen save

Type: developer, then tester. Owner decision 2026-11-18 (TASK/V61.md): both VDI depths, selectable at boot, 16
planes the default; ADR-038 is the contract for the 32-plane setting. Slice of the series V62-V69 cut from the

## Scope

ADR-038 points 5-7: the 32-plane device MFDB (cap raised to the setting's depth), the 32-plane standard form and
`vr_trnfm`, `screen_to_mem`/`mem_to_screen` and the save buffers as 32-bit copies, `v_get_pixel` with the high word in
`INTOUT[0]` and the low word in `INTOUT[1]`, a 16-plane memory MFDB refused at the 32 setting.

Sources: ADR-038 in `docs/MEMORY.md` (the contract; where it generalises an EmuTOS rule it cites the rule),
`docs/analysis/vdi-32bit-scope-2026-11-18.md` §1.2 (the change sites by file and function), TASK/V61.md step 2.
Before code: the spec files under `docs/api/vdi/` that this slice changes are extended for the 32-plane
setting (spec gate, researcher), since no EmuTOS path exists above 16 planes.

Depends on: V63.

## Acceptance

- A pixel built from the `vq_scrninfo` description, written with `vro_cpyfm` and read with `v_get_pixel`, comes back;
  `vr_trnfm` device to standard and back is the identity.
- A 16-plane MFDB at the 32 setting is a silent no-op; at the 16 setting the 16-plane contract of ADR-024 point 4 is
