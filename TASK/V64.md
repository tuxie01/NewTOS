## Scope

ADR-038 point 4 (pen quantisation, owner choice O2): pen to pixel `(c * 255 + 500) / 1000` per gun, `vq_color`
requested and actual values by the 8-bit rule; the 256 pens and their defaults stay.

Sources: ADR-038 in `docs/MEMORY.md` (the contract; where it generalises an EmuTOS rule it cites the rule),
`docs/analysis/vdi-32bit-scope-2026-11-18.md` §1.2 (the change sites by file and function), TASK/V61.md step 2.
Before code: the spec files under `docs/api/vdi/` that this slice changes are extended for the 32-plane
setting (spec gate, researcher), since no EmuTOS path exists above 16 planes.

Depends on: V63.

## Acceptance

  `vs_color`/`vq_color` as today.
- NewTOS at 16 against NewTOS at 32 on the same scenes, the 32-bit result projected to 16 bits: within one 5-bit step
  per gun (ADR-038 point 13 (ii)); the tester records the measured tolerance.
- At 16 planes nothing changes.
- `cargo xtask test` green; both board builds; warnings delta 0; doc-check of the edited docs 0 failures.

## Added from the V63 spec gate (lead, 2026-11-18)

With 8 bits per gun two pens can differ only in the low 3 bits of a gun and project to the same 16-bit word. The
flood-fill compare, `search_color` and `pixelread`'s return (`u16` today) therefore work on the masked 24-bit
pixel at 32 planes in this slice (rule R5 of `docs/api/vdi/raster/_raster.md`, section "32-plane setting: the
device pixel", and its boundary table); `read_pixel`'s reverse map through the palette follows the pens. At 16
planes all of them stay as they are. Acceptance adds: at 32 planes a contour fill stops at a pen that differs
from the fill pen only in the low 3 bits of one gun.

## Spec gate (researcher, 2026-11-18) and the lead's reading of its open points

Spec landed as `d35e67875`: `docs/api/vdi/raster/_raster.md`, section "32-plane setting: pens, and every place a
pen becomes a pixel" (rules P1-P9, the change-set table, rules F1-F5), with pointers in
`docs/api/vdi/attributes/_mode-clip-color.md`, `docs/api/vdi/inquiry/_color-pixel-input.md` and
`docs/api/vdi/output/_fill.md`; analysis
`docs/analysis/v64-contourfill-dumper-extent-and-acceptance-2026-11-18.md`.

Slice cut (lead; the acceptance criteria above are unchanged):

1. In scope beyond the list above: the device-global kernel palette and the `set_active_palette` trait signature,
   which does not fit 8-bit pens; the test doubles that implement the trait change with it (change-set table of the
   spec).
2. VT52 console colours (`tos-api/src/vdi/escape.rs`, the palette read of the cell writer): V64 changes the type of
   the workstation palette, so V64 keeps this site building and unchanged at 16 planes, and hands it the pen pixel as
   colour source at 32. The cell writer itself at 32 planes stays in TASK/V67.md.
3. `v_get_pixel` keeps the interim answer of TASK/V63.md (spec rule F5); the wide answer shape stays in TASK/V65.md
   (ADR-038 point 7).
4. The overlay-bit mask in the fill's colour path must not run at 32 planes (spec rule F3 area, marked as the
   researcher's inference): the developer checks it against the source and says in the receipt what was found.

Notes for the tester (method, not criteria):

- Test pens of the spec: requests (17,0,0) and (25,0,0) are one colour at 16 planes and two at 32.
- The 16-against-32 comparison is per gun on the top 5 bits, not on whole words: by the design of V63 bit 5 of the
  projected word differs on a cleared screen.
- The V63 run's "seed pixel only" was the case design (each dumper case starts with `v_clrwk`); a region extent is
  seen only inside one case (a rectangle grab) or by the host test.
