Type: research only (researcher). No product code, no ADR change in this ticket. Owner decision 2026-11-18,
from TASK/XBIOS24.md: "aber 64k farben ist doch nicht richtig. Lass mal Teradesk erstmal sein, lass uns wirklich

kannst du machen" (go).

## Facts

- The physical framebuffer is 32 bpp XRGB8888; the VDI workstation is a 16-plane RGB565 device over it
  (ADR-024). Programs therefore see 16 planes and 64K colours.
- ADR-024's reason: EmuTOS's truecolor VDI is bound to `v_planes > 8` and is only ever fed 16; EmuTOS has no
  24- or 32-bit VDI path. A 32-bit VDI has no EmuTOS specification.
- Since TASK/XBIOS22.md `VgetSize` returns the bytes of the 32-bpp screen and `VsetMode(-1)` answers `0x001C`
  (a 16-bit Falcon word); since TASK/XBIOS23.md `Getrez` answers 2.
- fVDI (`references/fvdi`) and MagiC are read-only comparisons for raster technique (ADR-035); neither is a
  specification today.
- The pinned Hatari oracle has the references tt and falcontc; falcontc is 16 bit.
- TeraDesk indexes a 17-entry table with the plane count (`docs/analysis/teradesk-video-pi-mode-2026-11-18.md`,
  section 3, side observation): 32 planes would read out of bounds.

## Questions

1. **Scope in NewTOS.** Which functions, tables and structures depend on the 16-plane device pixel: VDI
   (workstation open and `vq_extnd` values, attributes, colour mapping `vs_color`/`vq_color`, fills, lines,
   text, `vro_cpyfm`, `vrt_cpyfm`, `vr_trnfm`, `v_get_pixel`, escapes), the raster backend and its NEON kernels
   (ADR-036), the AES (`ap_planes`, icon and image conversion, resource recolouring), Line-A variables, XBIOS
   answers (`VsetMode`, `VgetSize`, `VgetRGB`), cookies (`_VDO`, `EdDI` if present), the screendump path. Give a
   list by file and function with the kind of change each needs.
2. **Source of the contract.** With no EmuTOS path: what would define the observable contract of a 32-plane
   device (pixel layout as programs see it, `vq_extnd`/`vq_scrninfo` answers, MFDB device format)? Which real
   Atari-side implementations exist to read (fVDI in `references/fvdi`; graphics-card VDIs named only as far as
   documented in sources at hand), and what would have to change in ADR-024 and ADR-035 for one to count as the
   specification. No behaviour from memory.
3. **Measurement.** What can still be compared against the pinned oracle at 32 planes, and what cannot; what
   would replace the pixel comparison where it is lost.
4. **Programs.** Which programs on the card and ports in `sdk/apps/` read the plane count or the device pixel
   format and what they do at 32 (zView, TeraDesk, QED, HighWire, hypview, the desktop in `programs/desktop/`):
   by source reading, with `file:line`; anything that needs a run is listed as "to be measured".
5. **Options.** At least: stay at 16 planes (status quo, what it costs in fidelity and speed); 32 planes for
   all; both selectable. Per option: what programs gain, the cost (number and kind of change sites from question
   1), what is thrown away, the fit to the roadmap (1.0 = the base like EmuTOS on the Pi, extensions after).

## Acceptance

- A dated analysis under `docs/analysis/` that answers the five questions, every claim cited (`file:line`, with
  symbol anchor into NewTOS code) or marked as inferred; the options compared without a decision.
- The owner decides from it whether and when a 32-bit VDI is built; that decision, not this ticket, changes any
  ADR.

3. `docs/DEVIATIONS.md` gets a row for today's state: the VDI reports 16 planes over a 32-bpp `Physbase` memory.
