# V61 — VDI at the framebuffer's real depth (32 bit): research of scope and effects

Status: OPEN (2026-11-18) | Priority: P2

Title: VDI at the framebuffer's real depth (32 bit): research of scope and effects

Type: research only (researcher). No product code, no ADR change in this ticket. Owner decision 2026-11-18,
from TASK/XBIOS24.md: "aber 64k farben ist doch nicht richtig. Lass mal Teradesk erstmal sein, lass uns wirklich
das implementieren wie es real ist. Also ins Betriebssystem. Wie wir das dann oben drauf anschauen ist erstmal
sekundär." (64K colours is not right; leave TeraDesk for now, let us implement it the way it really is, in the

kannst du machen" (go).

## Facts

- The physical framebuffer is 32 bpp XRGB8888; the VDI workstation is a 16-plane RGB565 device over it
  (ADR-024). Programs therefore see 16 planes and 64K colours.
- ADR-024's reason: EmuTOS's truecolor VDI is bound to `v_planes > 8` and is only ever fed 16; EmuTOS has no
  24- or 32-bit VDI path. A 32-bit VDI has no EmuTOS specification.
- Since TASK/XBIOS22.md `VgetSize` returns the bytes of the 32-bpp screen and `VsetMode(-1)` answers `0x001C`
  (a 16-bit Falcon word); since TASK/XBIOS23.md `Getrez` answers 2.
- fVDI (`references/fvdi`) and MagiC are read-only comparisons for raster technique (ADR-035); neither is a
  specification today.
- The pinned Hatari oracle has the references tt and falcontc; falcontc is 16 bit.
- TeraDesk indexes a 17-entry table with the plane count (`docs/analysis/teradesk-video-pi-mode-2026-11-18.md`,
  section 3, side observation): 32 planes would read out of bounds.

## Questions

1. **Scope in NewTOS.** Which functions, tables and structures depend on the 16-plane device pixel: VDI
   (workstation open and `vq_extnd` values, attributes, colour mapping `vs_color`/`vq_color`, fills, lines,
   text, `vro_cpyfm`, `vrt_cpyfm`, `vr_trnfm`, `v_get_pixel`, escapes), the raster backend and its NEON kernels
   (ADR-036), the AES (`ap_planes`, icon and image conversion, resource recolouring), Line-A variables, XBIOS
   answers (`VsetMode`, `VgetSize`, `VgetRGB`), cookies (`_VDO`, `EdDI` if present), the screendump path. Give a
   list by file and function with the kind of change each needs.
2. **Source of the contract.** With no EmuTOS path: what would define the observable contract of a 32-plane
   device (pixel layout as programs see it, `vq_extnd`/`vq_scrninfo` answers, MFDB device format)? Which real
   Atari-side implementations exist to read (fVDI in `references/fvdi`; graphics-card VDIs named only as far as
   documented in sources at hand), and what would have to change in ADR-024 and ADR-035 for one to count as the
   specification. No behaviour from memory.
3. **Measurement.** What can still be compared against the pinned oracle at 32 planes, and what cannot; what
   would replace the pixel comparison where it is lost.
4. **Programs.** Which programs on the card and ports in `sdk/apps/` read the plane count or the device pixel
   format and what they do at 32 (zView, TeraDesk, QED, HighWire, hypview, the desktop in `programs/desktop/`):
   by source reading, with `file:line`; anything that needs a run is listed as "to be measured".
5. **Options.** At least: stay at 16 planes (status quo, what it costs in fidelity and speed); 32 planes for
   all; both selectable. Per option: what programs gain, the cost (number and kind of change sites from question
   1), what is thrown away, the fit to the roadmap (1.0 = the base like EmuTOS on the Pi, extensions after).

sensible answer: the alternatives and their cost are listed in section B, nothing is picked), **[M]** needs a

### A. The three open points of step 1

**Point 1: fVDI's 32-bit `vr_trnfm` (closed [C]).** Read to the end: `references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:985-1521`.

- The routine selects the chunky branch from the device's format word (`dev_format & 2`, `references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1048-1054`)
  and then by the MFDB plane count: 16 -> `to_standard_16`, 8 -> `to_standard_8`, 32 -> `to_standard_32`
  (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1057-1086`; the in-place copy repeats it, `references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1322-1352`).
- `to_standard_32` (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1188-1237`) loads 16 pixels (64 bytes, four `movem.l` pairs,
  `references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1203-1209`) into a work area and runs 32 plane iterations (`moveq #31,d3`,
  `references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1211`). In each it walks the 16 longs from the last (offset 60) to the first
  (`moveq #60,d4`, `references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1214`), shifts the **top bit** of each long out with `add.l d5,d5`
  and collects it with `roxr.w #1,d6` (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1216-1224`), so after 16 steps the first
  pixel's bit is bit 15 of the word and the last pixel's is bit 0. The word is stored, then the destination pointer
  advances by `d7` = one plane (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1226-1227`, `d7` from `:1057-1059`).
- Result **[C]**: standard plane 0 holds bit 31 of every pixel, plane 31 holds bit 0; planes follow one another
  (plane-sequential, `h * wdwidth` words per plane); inside a word the leftmost pixel is the most significant bit.
  The 16-bit branch is the same code with 16 planes (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1142-1185`), and EmuTOS's
  16-bit form is the same shape: plane 0 is the `0x8000` bit of the pixel word, `planesize = fd_h * fd_wdwidth` words per
  plane, `formsize = planesize * 2 * 16` bytes (`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_raster.c:190-199,218-232`). So the 32-plane
  standard form is the 16-plane one with 32 planes and a 32-bit pixel word **[I]** (same bit rule, two sources agree on
  the plane order).
- Standard -> device (`finish_up`, `references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1400-1520`): the 32-bit branch
  (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1461-1518`) rebuilds a pixel by 32 `roxr.l #1,d6` steps from plane 31 up to
  plane 0, so plane 0 ends as bit 31: the exact inverse **[C]**. The 16-bit case reuses `to_standard_16` as "its own
  inverse" (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1416`).
- The pixel is a machine **long as the CPU reads it** (`move.l 0(a6,d4),d5`, `references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1217`):
  fVDI defines the form on the integer value, not on memory bytes, and its own source doubts the routine
  (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1100,1190`: "Doesn't seem to work correctly (Jinnee)"). The draft therefore takes
  the form from the EmuTOS 16-bit rule extended to 32 bits and uses fVDI only as the second witness of the plane order.
- Leftover: the palette pointers `finish_up` loads in the 32-bit branch
  (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1465-1474`) are not used in the loop shown
  (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:1476-1518`) **[C]**; nothing is concluded from them.

**Point 2: the "Intel byte order" flag (closed as far as sources reach; the rest is decided by consumer behaviour [I]).**

- What the sources say: fVDI documents `org` as `0x01` usual bit order and `0x80` Intel byte order
  (`references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/spec.c:60-62`, `references/fvdi/fvdi/docs/driver.txt:366-368`), and describes
  `0x81` as "Falcon 5+6+5 bit order, but Intel byte order" (`references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/include/driver.h:59`) **[C]**.
  The 16-bit Falcon row of the ARAnyM driver carries `r_16f/g_16f/b_16f` with `org 1`, the 16-, 24- and 32-bit
  "fb layout" rows carry `0x81` (`references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/spec.c:72-80`), and the table's own comment says
  "FIXME: big and little endian differences" (`references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/spec.c:64`) **[C]**. fVDI therefore
  documents the flag but does not decide what a little-endian store announces.
- NVDI's description, as already extracted in `docs/api/vdi/eddi-vq_scrninfo.md` §3.3, lists for 32 planes
  `Bit 0 = xxxxxxxx-rrrrrrrr-gggggggg-bbbbbbbb` and `Bit 7 = bbbbbbbb-gggggggg-rrrrrrrr-xxxxxxxx` (swapped)
  **[C]** (read in that file; the NVDI text itself is not in the tree). fVDI's `32` "usual" row has red 16..23, green
  8..15, blue 0..7 with `org 1` (`references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/spec.c:25-27,75`) **[C]**: the two agree on the
  `0xXXRRGGBB` numbering for flag 1.
- What programs do: HighWire and zView derive `reverse` and `z_trail` from the bit numbers only (`out[16] < out[48]`,
  `out[48] > 0`), not from `[14]` (`sdk/apps/highwire/src/raster.c:1713-1721`,
  `Applications/z-tools/zview/zvdi/vdi.c:38-41`); with red 16..23 and blue 0..7 both are false and they take the plain
  32-bit path, which stores `(r << 16 | g << 8 | b)` as one native long (`sdk/apps/highwire/src/raster.c:1392-1405`)
  **[C]**. On a little-endian store a native `0x00RRGGBB` is the byte sequence B, G, R, 0, which is the XRGB8888 store
  (`kernel/src/hal/framebuffer.rs:61@pixels` and its module doc) **[I]**: the device pixel and the store word are the same
  integer.
- Draft answer **[D]**: announce `[14] = 1` (usual), numbering **logical** (red 16..23, green 8..15, blue 0..7), i.e.
  the same convention NewTOS already uses at 16 planes (red 11..15 for a native `u16`,
  `tos-api/src/vdi/eddi.rs:112@scrninfo_table`; that choice is E3 of the EdDI spec). The `0x81` row of fVDI is **not**
  adopted: it belongs to ARAnyM's host framebuffer with its own FIXME. A future m68k thunk (big-endian programs reading a
  long) would see swapped bytes; that is the thunk's problem and is listed in the open points.

**Point 3: the X byte (not decidable from sources -> owner choice O1).** EmuTOS has no 32-plane path and no X byte
(`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_defs.h:22`, ADR-024 context) **[C]**. NVDI's text calls the top eight bits
"Overlay/ungenutzt" (`docs/api/vdi/eddi-vq_scrninfo.md` §3.3) **[C]**. fVDI reports no alpha, genlock or unused bits for
32 planes (`none` in all three slots, `references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/spec.c:75`) and counts `1 << (r+g+b bits)` =
2^24 colours (`references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/common/init.c:123-128`) **[C]**: the X byte is simply unreported. Today's
NewTOS store keeps the 16-bit overlay bit there, replicated into eight bits (`kernel/src/vdi_backend.rs:192@OVERLAY_X`),
and tells the VideoCore to ignore alpha (`kernel/src/drivers/bcm2711/framebuffer.rs:60@ALPHA_MODE_IGNORED`) **[C]**. At
32 planes there is no EmuTOS overlay bit to carry, so what X means is a choice (section B, O1).

### B. Owner choices and measurements still open

**O1. What the X byte of the 32-plane pixel is.** Everything below depends on it: `v_clrwk` fill, bitwise raster
operations, `v_get_pixel` and flood-fill reads, the `vq_scrninfo` words 12/13 and the tables 80..127.

| | A. unused, masked | B. overlay carrier, as at 16 planes | C. alpha |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pixel written by pens | `0x00RRGGBB` | `0x00RRGGBB` (bit clear, as `rgb()` today, `kernel/src/hal/framebuffer.rs:61@pixels`) | needs a value (`0xFF` or `0x00`, by the firmware mode) |
| `v_clrwk` | EmuTOS fills every byte `0xff` for truecolor (`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_control.c:610-618`): `0xFFFFFFFF` is a legal write; reads mask X, so clear equals white pen | same fill; clear (`0xFFFFFFFF`) and white pen (`0x00FFFFFF`) stay two pixels, as `0xFFFF` and `0xFFDF` do at 16 planes (`kernel/src/vdi_backend.rs:200@word_to_device`) | a blank screen is not white unless the alpha mode is chosen to show it |
| Raster logic ops, `WM_XOR` | bitwise on 32 bits; X noise ignored on read | bitwise on 32 bits; X result is meaningful (overlay) | X result changes what the display shows |
| `v_get_pixel`, flood fill | X masked off (EmuTOS masks the overlay bit on read, `references/emutos/vdi/vdi_fill.c:702-709,755`) | X returned unmasked as `pixelread` does at 16 (`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_fill.c:671-681`) | not applicable |
| `vq_scrninfo` | `[13] = 8`, unused table `[96..103] = 24..31`, `[12] = 0` | `[12] = 8`, genlock table `[80..87] = 24..31`, `[13] = 0`; a program that sets one genlock bit needs a defined meaning for "overlay = the X byte" (no source defines it) | `[11] = 8`, alpha table `[64..71] = 24..31`; the VideoCore alpha mode must change from "ignored" |
| Cost | fewest changes: read paths mask; `bb_save` and copies are plain 32-bit | keeps today's `word_to_device` rule alive at 32 planes; most new rules | firmware alpha behaviour is documented only as three modes (`kernel/src/drivers/bcm2711/framebuffer.rs:60@ALPHA_MODE_IGNORED` doc); display blending untested |

**O2. Pen quantisation at 32 planes.** EmuTOS packs 5 bits per gun with `(c * 31 + 500) / 1000`
(`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_col.c:586-590,594-616`). fVDI's 32-bit driver uses the same formula with 8 bits,
`(c * ((1 << 8) - 1) + 500) / 1000` (`references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/palette.c:194` with `red_bits 8`,
`references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/32b_pal.c:10-13`) **[C]**.
- (a) **8 bits per gun**: meets the owner's "64K colours is not right"; 256 levels. Consequence **[computed]**: the
  5-bit projection of an 8-bit pen differs from EmuTOS's word for 214 of the 1001 request values 0..1000 when taking
  the top five bits, for 30 when rescaling `(q8 * 31 + 127) / 255`; the largest difference in either projection is one
  5-bit step (my arithmetic over `c = 0..1000`, no run). The projection comparison of step 1 §3.2 is then exact only up
  to that tolerance **[M]**.
- (b) **5 bits then replicated to 8**: exact projection (the store is what it is today), 32768 distinct pen colours,
  which does not meet the owner's reason for the change; no source asks for it.

**O3. `v_get_pixel` answer at 32 planes.** The pixel needs two words. EmuTOS returns `INTOUT[0] = 0`, `INTOUT[1] = pel`
(`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_fill.c:1104-1110`); fVDI returns the low word in `INTOUT[0]` and, above 16 bits, the high
word in `INTOUT[1]`, else `-1` (`references/fvdi/fvdi/engine/blit.s:44-96`, the 16-bit test at `:82-86`) **[C]**.
- (a) **EmuTOS shape widened**: `INTOUT[0]` = high word, `INTOUT[1]` = low word. At 16 planes it is EmuTOS's answer
  unchanged (high word 0).
- (b) **fVDI shape at 32 planes only**: low word first. At the default 16 planes NewTOS must keep EmuTOS's shape (the
  oracle), so the two settings would order the words differently.
No source in the tree says what a real 32-bit consumer of `v_get_pixel` expects.

**O4. `VsetMode(-1)` in the 32-plane setting.** The Falcon mode word has a 3-bit depth field whose largest value is
`VIDEL_TRUECOLOR = 4` (`references/emutos/include/biosdefs.h:146-154`), and EmuTOS clamps a larger field to truecolor
(`references/emutos/bios/videl.c:733-738`) **[C]**. No Falcon word names 32 bits.
- (a) keep `0x001C` in both settings (`tos-api/src/xbios/screen.rs:442@FALCON_MODE_WORD`): programs that gate on the
  Falcon word keep working; the word says "16-bit truecolor" while the VDI says 32 planes (TeraDesk maps depth 4 to 16
  planes, `docs/api/xbios/58-vsetmode.md:61`).
- (b) answer a value outside the Falcon word space: no source defines one; Falcon-aware code that masks with
  `VIDEL_VALID` (`references/emutos/include/biosdefs.h:144`) would read an invalid or clamped mode.

**O5. A 16-plane memory MFDB in the 32-plane setting.** ADR-024 point 4 makes the 16-bit memory MFDB contract. EmuTOS
refuses more than 16 planes for the blit (`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_raster.c:962-966`) and has no other depth to convert
to **[C]**. `present` hardwires `fd_nplanes = 16` (`sdk/apps/present/present.c:620@fd_nplanes`).
- (a) **refuse** (silent no-op, as EmuTOS does for an unsupported plane count): simplest; `present` and any 16-bit
  program then needs the 16-plane setting or a patch.
- (b) **convert** RGB565 <-> the 32-bit pixel at the seam: keeps 16-bit-hardwired programs drawing, but the `u16` seam
  (`tos-api/src/vdi/backend.rs:138@pel_to_device16`) and the overlay rule stay alive in the 32-plane setting, and what a
  16-bit word means for O1 and O2 has to be defined.

**To be measured [M]** (none made): (1) the no-op of a 32-plane `vro_cpyfm` for TeraDesk, HighWire, hypview with today's
cap (`tos-api/src/vdi/raster.rs:821@CHUNKY_PLANES`); (2) the projection tolerance of O2(a) on text, fills and lines;
(3) Fbench of the seam against a plain 32-bit copy (ADR-036 point 5); (4) zView's `1 << 32` path
(`Applications/z-tools/zview/zvdi/color.c:75`).

### C. ADR draft (the lead assigns the number and applies it)

**Title:** VDI device depth: 16 planes by default, 32 planes by a boot setting.
**Status:** Proposed (owner decision 2026-11-18, TASK/V61.md: option C, "ich gehe mit deinen Empfehlungen").
**Phase:** cross-cutting (VDI workstation, raster backend, AES `ap_planes`, EdDI, XBIOS Falcon-video answers).

**Context.** ADR-024 made the VDI a 16-plane RGB565 device over a 32-bpp store; the store is 32-bpp XRGB8888 by the
framebuffer request (`kernel/src/drivers/bcm2711/framebuffer.rs:34@DEPTH_BPP`), the device word is 16 bits
(`kernel/src/vdi_backend.rs:44@PLANES`). `Physbase` already hands out 32-bpp memory
(`sdk/examples/physbase.c:19-25@XRGB8888`), `VgetSize` already counts it (`tos-api/src/xbios/screen.rs:482@vgetsize`).
EmuTOS has no 24/32-bit VDI path (`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_defs.h:22`, ADR-024). The owner wants the VDI to report the
depth the store really has.

**Decision.**

1. **Two depths, one default.** The VDI workstation has 16 planes (ADR-024 unchanged, the oracle-measurable mode,
   default) or 32 planes. 32 is the same store with a 32-bit device pixel. No other depth (24 packed has no store).
2. **Selection.** The boot argument `vdiplanes=<16|32>` in `cmdline.txt`, read once at boot with the existing mechanism:
   `bootargs::numeric_setting` (`kernel/src/bootargs.rs:113@numeric_setting`) through `boot_switch`
   (`kernel/src/lib.rs:288@mdlimit`, `:296@input_aes`), absent = 16; any other value counts as `Setting::Invalid`
   (`kernel/src/bootargs.rs:98@Setting`) and gives 16 with a boot line **[D]** (the token name is cosmetic). It is a boot
   argument, not a call, so a program cannot change the depth (as for `mdlimit`, `kernel/src/bootargs.rs:94@MDLIMIT`
   doc). No run-time switch: ADR-017 Revision 2 has none, and `shel_write(SHW_RESCHNG)` records the request without
   switching (`tos-api/src/aes/shell.rs:484-492@SHW_RESCHNG`) **[C]**. `config.txt` is the firmware's file and the kernel
   reads the geometry from the VideoCore, not from it (ADR-017 Revision 2) **[C]**; `NEWDESK.INF` belongs to the
   desktop, which runs after the VDI exists **[I]**: `cmdline.txt` is the channel the kernel already reads for its boot
   switches. ADR-017's "config surface = exactly two files" is amended by one sentence.
3. **What the program sees at 32 planes** (through the same code paths as at 16; `planes()` becomes a boot-time value
   instead of a constant): `vq_extnd` `INQ_TAB[4] = 32` (`tos-api/src/vdi/workstation.rs:325@inq`); `INQ_TAB[5] = 0`, no
   video LUT (EmuTOS writes `== 16` only because 16 is the only truecolor depth it knows,
   `references/emutos/vdi/vdi_control.c:211-213`; fVDI's driver writes 0 for every depth,
   `references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/spec.c:389`; today's gate `tos-api/src/vdi/workstation.rs:330@planes`) **[D]**;
   `DEV_TAB[13] = 256` and `DEV_TAB[35] = 1` by EmuTOS's formulas (`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_control.c:206-207`);
   `DEV_TAB[39] = 0` (`tos-api/src/vdi/workstation.rs:39@palette_precision`); `ap_planes = 32`
   (`tos-api/src/aes/appl.rs:369@screen_planes`); the `planes > 8` truecolor gates stay true
   (`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_defs.h:22`). The 256 pens, their default values and the default pen set stay (ADR-024
   point 2); only their quantisation depends on O2.
4. **Device pixel.** One 32-bit integer `0xXXRRGGBB`, 8 bits per gun, equal to the store word (little-endian bytes B, G,
   R, X). X: O1. Pen to pixel, **if O2(a)**: `q = (c * 255 + 500) / 1000` per gun for `c` clamped to 0..1000 (the EmuTOS
   rule with 8 bits, `references/emutos/vdi/vdi_col.c:586-590,594-616`; fVDI
   `references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/palette.c:194`) **[I]**; `vq_color` returns the last-requested values
   (`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_col.c:821-826`) or, for "actual", `(q * 1000 + 127) / 255` (the EmuTOS rule
   `(q * 1000 + 16) / 31`, `references/emutos/vdi/vdi_col.c:800-805`, with 8 bits; fVDI
   `references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/aranym/palette.c:195`) **[I]**.
5. **Memory MFDB.** `fd_stand = 0`, `fd_nplanes = 32`, one native-endian 32-bit pixel per position, a row of
   `fd_wdwidth * 16` pixels (analogue of ADR-024 point 4, where a row is `fd_wdwidth * 16` native 16-bit words,
   `references/emutos/vdi/vdi_raster.c:190-199`) **[D]**. `vro_cpyfm` and `vrt_cpyfm` accept 32-plane device MFDBs (the
   cap `plane_ct <= 16`, `references/emutos/vdi/vdi_raster.c:962-966`, `tos-api/src/vdi/raster.rs:821@CHUNKY_PLANES`, is
   raised to the setting's depth); logic operations are bitwise on 32 bits as EmuTOS's are bitwise on 16
   (`references/emutos/vdi/vdi_raster.c:975-985`) **[I]**. A 16-plane MFDB in this setting: O5.
6. **Standard form and `vr_trnfm`.** 32 planes, plane-sequential, `fd_h * fd_wdwidth` words per plane, plane `p` holds
   bit `31 - p` of the pixel, leftmost pixel = most significant bit of the word, total `planesize * 32` words;
   transform in place through a scratch buffer, `fd_stand` of the destination left to the caller, a failed allocation
   silent (all as `vr_trnfm16`, `references/emutos/vdi/vdi_raster.c:190-258`, with 32 for 16; point 1 above for fVDI)
   **[I]**. The truecolor gate (`src.nplanes > 8` and `planes() > 8`) already holds
   (`tos-api/src/vdi/raster.rs:1523@planes`).
7. **`v_get_pixel`.** O3. X per O1.
8. **`vq_scrninfo` / EdDI.** The answer is generated for both depths (today only at 16,
   `tos-api/src/vdi/eddi.rs:82@DEVICE_PLANES`). At 32 planes: `[0] = 2` (packed), `[1] = 2` (software CLUT), `[2] = 32`,
   `[3] = 0x0100`, `[4] = 0` (2^24 colours as a LONG, high word first; fVDI
   `references/fvdi/fvdi/drivers/common/init.c:123-128`, NVDI "16,7 Mio." for 32 planes,
   `docs/api/vdi/eddi-vq_scrninfo.md` §3.3), `[5..7] = 0` (as at 16), `[8..10] = 8`, `[14] = 1`, red `[16..23] = 16..23`,
   green `[32..39] = 8..15`, blue `[48..55] = 0..7`, `-1` elsewhere in 16..127 except the X entries (O1),
   `[128..272] = 0`. Structural basis: the existing table (`tos-api/src/vdi/eddi.rs:112@scrninfo_table`) and the spec
   file above; value basis: NVDI statement via that spec and fVDI's `32` "usual" row (point 2 above) **[D]**. The `EdDI`
   cookie is the same marker (`tos-api/src/cookie.rs:37@EDDI`).
9. **XBIOS and cookies at 32 planes.** `VgetSize`: unchanged (already the 32-bpp store; it now agrees with the VDI).
   `Getrez`: 2 (the answer depends on the mode word having `VIDEL_COMPAT` clear,
   `tos-api/src/xbios/screen.rs:180@getrez`; ADR-017 Revision 3). `VgetRGB`, `VgetMonitor`, `_VDO = VDO_FALCON`
   (`tos-api/src/cookie.rs:266@VDO_FALCON`), `_MCH`: unchanged. `VsetMode(-1)`: O4. At 16 planes all of them stay as they
   are; `VgetSize` keeps its row in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`.
10. **AES.** `ap_planes = 32`; the screen save buffer (`tos-api/src/aes/gsxif.rs:354-355@bb_save_words`) doubles in size
    (arithmetic, step 1 §1.2); colour-icon device forms follow the MFDB of point 5
    (`tos-api/src/aes/resource.rs:3058@CiconDevice`); the file-side `num_planes` of resources is the file's depth and is
    not touched.
11. **Raster kernels** (ADR-036): `fill_span`, `raster_op`, expansion and line kernels keep working on `u32` pixels; only
    the pen-to-pixel step, the clear value and the read-back masks change **[I]**. The `u16` seam
    (`tos-api/src/vdi/backend.rs:138@pel_to_device16` and the trait methods of step 1 §1.1) stays for the 16-plane
    setting.
12. **What counts as the specification where EmuTOS has none (amendments).**
    - ADR-035 point 1 gets an exception: for a depth EmuTOS does not have (32 planes) the contract is this ADR; EmuTOS
      still decides every pixel at 16 planes and every rule that generalises mechanically (points 3-6 cite the rule they
      generalise). Point 2 additionally permits reading *values of a documented table* (point 8) from fVDI as a
      plausibility witness, not as the source. Point 3 stands: nothing copied; the table is written from this ADR's
      description. Point 4 gets a carve-out: at 32 planes the oracle cannot decide.
    - ADR-024 points 1, 4, 5 read "at the 16-plane setting"; point 3 gains: at 32 planes the store word is the device
      pixel and its X byte is visible as O1 says.
    - ADR-017 Revision 2 "config surface" gains the boot-argument sentence of point 2 of this draft.
    - `docs/COMPATIBILITY.md` gets one section for decision points 3-9 (what is contract, what is own design); the rows of
      `VsetMode`/`VgetSize`/`Getrez` are revised by O4.
13. **Testing without the oracle** (step 1 §3.3, tightened):
    - The default (16) keeps the whole existing tosdump/tosdiff regression bar, unchanged and still against
      `tt`/`falcontc`.
    - 32 planes: (i) a structural self-test of the `vq_scrninfo` table (bit tables partition the word, colour count
      `1 << (r+g+b)`, a built pixel through `vro_cpyfm` then `v_get_pixel` comes back; the round trip the EdDI spec itself
      asks for, `docs/api/vdi/eddi-vq_scrninfo.md` §6); (ii) NewTOS-16 against NewTOS-32 on the same scenes with the
      32-bit result projected to 16 bits: exact under O2(b), within one 5-bit step per gun under O2(a) (computed above)
      **[M]**; (iii) program-level acceptance (HighWire, zView, DOOM show a correct image) **[M]**; (iv) the tosdump caps at
      16 planes are lifted for 32 (`tosdump/core/cases.c:2548@planes`, `tosdump/core/cases.c:2176@nplanes`,
      `tosdump/core/dumprast.c:128@planes`), with the 32-plane expected values coming from points 3-8 of this ADR,
      labelled "NewTOS contract", never "oracle".

**Consequences.** Programs at risk in the 32 setting: TeraDesk (`npc[]` has 17 entries, indexed by the plane count,
`sdk/apps/teradesk/video.c:446,592`: an out-of-bounds read at 32), `present` (hardwired 16, O5), hypview and any program
that stamps `vq_extnd[4]` into a memory MFDB until the cap is raised (step 1 §4). They keep working at the default 16. The
`docs/DEVIATIONS.md` row "VDI says 16 planes over a 32-bpp `Physbase`" is removed when the 32 setting becomes the
default; it stays while 16 is the default.

### Open points

STOP-blocks / owner choices (section B): O1 X byte, O2 pen quantisation, O3 `v_get_pixel` shape, O4 `VsetMode(-1)` word,
O5 16-plane MFDB in the 32 setting. O1 and O2 decide the tests of decision point 13; O3-O5 decide the answers programs see.

To be measured: the four items of section B "To be measured".

Inferred (labelled [I] above): the 32-plane standard form as the 16-plane rule extended; that a native `0x00RRGGBB` equals

logic ops bitwise on 32 bits; that the kernels need no change beyond the pen, clear and read steps.
